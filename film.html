1980s

Writing, assistant-directing and script-editing on feature films including Coda, Fever, Strangers and The Dreaming. Screenplay Tomorrow’s Journey nominated for the Monte Miller Award and Goldspinner series broadcast on ABC children’s television.

1990s

Family feature Dark Side of the Pumpkin funded for development by the SAFC with Script Editor Andrew Bovell (Lantana).

1990’s/2000

Supported by the ACTF and SAFC, Steph develops her published novels for young adults, Diary Z and Red into a bible for a TV series.

2008

18 min ‘no budget’ film Flip’d! premieres at the South Australian Film Corporation.

2010

Stage play Frank’ll Kill Me morphs to feature screenplay Mary Mary, selected for the Screen NSW 2009 Aurora Workshops.

On-going

Thriller Mary Mary, family feature film Desert Train, comedy Dark Side of the Pumpkin
